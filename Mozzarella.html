Mozzarella

Stretching:Mozzarella gets its signature texture from a traditional process called pasta filata, were fresh curds are heated in hot water and stretched till smooth

Milk:Authentic Mozzarella is made from the milk of domestic water buffalo rather than cows.

Melting:Low-moisture mozzarella is specifically aged longer to remove water, turning golden brown when heated making it perfect for pizza.

Name:The word mozzarella comes from the Neapolitan word mozzare, which means "to cut off" and refers to the cheesemaker pinching off individual balls of curd by hand
