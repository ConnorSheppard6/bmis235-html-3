Burrata

Structure:Burrata consists of a solid outer pouch madeof stretched mozzarella that hides a soft, creamy center of shredded curds and fresh cream called stracciatella

Origins:Cheesemakers in the Puglia region of southern Italy invented burrata in the early 20th century as a clever way to useup leftover scraps frm mozzarella

Protection:Authentic Burrata holds special European protected status, only being allowed to be produced in Puglia region

Meaning:Burrata translates to "buttered" in Italian, which describes its interior rather than actual ingredients
